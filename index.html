Before You Join

Our project is an ever-expanding recreation of South Dakota Mines and Rapid City in Minecraft! It's open to anyone, Bedrock or Java, local or not, to help us expand further downtown, furnish buildings, or simply look around. Join the Discord server or read further to learn more!

Here are the rules. Please (please) don't do anything that would make us want to add another one.

Feel Free to Help

If you'd like to contribute, we need all the help we can get. Every block counts!

Treat Others with Respect

Please be kind; any kind of hateful or offensive conduct will not be tolerated.

Treat the Project with Respect

Do not intentionally disrupt or otherwise cause harm to the build.

You Can Have Fun

You may make unrelated builds as long as they don't interfere with the project.
